# Code of Conduct

Apranik follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies in full to every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions and any channel run in the project's name.

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and do not harass,
insult or discriminate. People using Apranik live under real risk, so do not ask
anyone to reveal who they are, where they are or how they connect.

## Reporting

Write to **we-apranik@proton.me**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people
who break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
